Case Study — Client Thumbnails Package

Design process and learning outcomes

Context: One-time client requested a set of thumbnails and promotional images for online content. My role: design and deliver variations in multiple aspect ratios suitable for YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram.

Process

  1. Gathered client brand colors, target audience, and tone preferences.
  2. Prepared 3 initial mockups in Canva / Photoshop for feedback.
  3. Iterated on typography, contrast, and focal points; delivered final PNGs and source files.
  4. Provided scaled versions and simple guidelines for reuse.

Tools used

Canva Adobe Photoshop Adobe Illustrator

Outcome & Learning

Client was satisfied; the package improved click-through rates on thumbnails slightly (informal client feedback). This project strengthened my composition and rapid iteration skills.
